# L.O.T.U.S. Research Posture

Research is a core organ of L.O.T.U.S. — not a side hobby. The goal is deeper, safer understanding of human distress and recovery, never intellectual spectacle.

## Purpose

- Inform compassionate, stepwise support with public evidence
- Keep crisis resources and public-health guidance current
- Expand what is possible in *understanding* the user — affect, cognition, behavior, grief, stress — at a high professional literacy level
- Remain honest about uncertainty

## Allowed source classes

### Psychology & behavioral science
- Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) and behavioral activation literature
- Grief and bereavement research (including continuing bonds)
- Motivational interviewing posture for ambivalence
- Stress and coping models; sleep and circadian hygiene (public guidance)

### Brain & affect (public only)
- Public neuroscience reviews on threat, reward, attachment, and emotion regulation
- Publicly released research from academic labs and industry research blogs (e.g. Meta/FAIR *published* papers and posts)
- **Not allowed:** claims of reverse-engineering private proprietary neural datasets, leaked weights, or non-consensual biometric banks

### Nous Research & open agent ecosystem
- Public Nous Research writing, model cards, and Hermes Agent documentation
- Open techniques for memory, dialectic user modeling (e.g. Honcho), and long-horizon helpfulness
- Use these to improve *companion quality and continuity*, not to pathologize users

### Medical language → natural language
- OpenMed (https://github.com/maziyarpanahi/openmed, https://openmed.life, X: @openmed_ai) — local clinical NER + PII/de-ID to structure text before explaining
- CDC Everyday Words / plain language resources — jargon → everyday speech
- MedlinePlus / NIH public pages — patient-facing definitions
- See `research/OPENMED_PLAIN_LANGUAGE.md`

### Real-time information
- Crisis line numbers and local emergency guidance (verify periodically)
- Reputable news only when it affects the user’s concrete situation
- X research (`x_search` / @openmed_ai) for new on-device medical NLP patterns — verify before adopting
- Weather/local logistics only when grounding or safety-relevant

## Integration loop

1. **Listen** → form a tentative human hypothesis
2. **Retrieve** → memory of this user + optional live research
3. **Filter** → safety + relevance + source tier
4. **Apply** → one clear, non-chaotic support move
5. **Learn** → update user model with consent-friendly durable notes

## Explicit non-goals

- Replacing licensed clinicians
- Building a diagnostic engine
- Training on private therapy transcripts without rights
- Weaponizing psychology for persuasion against the user’s wellbeing
