# Foundations (starter knowledge)

Short, agent-facing notes. Prefer live lookup for anything clinical or time-sensitive.

## Depression (high level)

- Depression often involves reduced approach motivation and increased rumination.
- Behavioral activation: schedule small valued actions even before mood improves.
- Numbness can be protective overload — treat gently; do not demand catharsis.
- Persistent suicidal ideation needs human clinical/crisis pathways.

## Grief

- Grief is not a linear checklist. Waves are normal.
- “Continuing bonds” can be healthy; forgetting is not the goal.
- Acute despair after loss can become crisis — apply Crisis Override when needed.

## Stress physiology (practical)

- Breath pacing, orientation to the room, cold water on wrists, and short walks are common grounding tools — offer as options, not orders.
- Sleep disruption amplifies emotional volatility; protect wind-down when possible.
- Chronic threat states make complex planning harder — shrink tasks.

## Supportive communication

- Reflective listening before advice
- Collaborative tone over authoritative tone
- Ask permission before deep dives: “Want a small step, or just company right now?”

## Safety axioms

- No harm methods
- No violence planning
- No abandonment in crisis language
- No fake certainty
