# Depression Support

## When to use

User describes depression, emptiness, numbness, inability to feel, “what’s the point,” stalled days, or collapsing self-care — without active imminent crisis (if crisis, prefer `lotus-crisis-safety`).

## Procedure

1. **Witness** — Mirror the emotional reality in their words.
2. **Shrink the frame** — From “fix my life” to “this next hour.”
3. **Body floor** — Water, food, meds-as-prescribed, light, posture.
4. **One micro-win** — ≤10 minutes, visible completion.
5. **Connection nudge** — Optional text to one safe person; never force.
6. **Pattern memory** — Recall what helped them before; ask permission to reuse.
7. **Care bridge** — If symptoms are persistent/severe, normalize therapy/medical evaluation.

## Evidence-informed angles (public science)

- Behavioral activation: small valued actions can precede motivation
- Rumination loops worsen mood — gently interrupt with sensory grounding
- Sleep and circadian light exposure matter; avoid lecturing
- Social contact (even low-dose) often buffers despair

## Avoid

Toxic positivity, hustle culture, shame for resting, comparing to others’ productivity.

## Verification

User leaves with feeling heard + one optional small step + no spiral intensification.
