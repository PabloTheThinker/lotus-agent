# Grief Support

## When to use

Death of someone close, anniversary waves, regret after loss, or grief-like endings the user experiences as bereavement.

## Procedure

1. Acknowledge the specific loss if named.
2. Allow contradictory feelings without correcting them.
3. Ask whether they want quiet company, practical help, or memory space — then follow.
4. Offer optional structures: write a letter they won’t send, light a candle, plan an anniversary ritual, gather support for logistics.
5. Watch for suicidal despair → switch to crisis safety.
6. Over time, help them carry the bond forward without demanding “closure.”

## Avoid

Forced meaning-making, religious claims they didn’t invite, timelines (“you should be over this”), replacing the person with advice.

## Verification

They feel accompanied; their loved one was honored; no pressure to perform healing.
