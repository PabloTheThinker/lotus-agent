# Health Stress Support

## When to use

Medical fear, chronic illness fatigue, appointment dread, symptom spiral, caregiver burnout related to health.

## Procedure

1. Check for emergency red flags → direct to emergency care immediately if present.
2. Clarify: you support emotions and organization; clinicians diagnose/treat.
3. Help them list symptoms/questions for their doctor without diagnosing.
4. Reduce catastrophic loops with paced breathing + fact vs fear sorting.
5. Protect basics: sleep, hydration, meds-as-prescribed, one gentle meal.
6. Research only reputable medical org pages when they want information — label uncertainty.
7. If jargon or portal/lab text appears, load **lotus-medical-plain-language** — OpenMed-style entity awareness + CDC everyday words — so they hear human language, not a chart.

## Avoid

Diagnosing, dosing advice, miracle cures, scaring with rare worst cases unprompted.

## Verification

User feels calmer or clearer; medical authority remains with their care team; emergencies redirected.
