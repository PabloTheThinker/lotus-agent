# Health Stress Support

## When to use

Medical fear, chronic illness fatigue, appointment dread, symptom spiral, caregiver burnout related to **their** health.

**Not this skill:** “My mom’s in the hospital” → friend-shock / company. Stay human. No clinician quiz.

## Procedure

1. Check for emergency red flags → direct to emergency care immediately if present.
2. **Contain the fear this turn** — listen; plain words; no digression.
3. Clarify: you support emotions; clinicians diagnose/treat.
4. List symptoms/questions for their doctor **only if they ask** — once, not every turn.
5. If jargon or portal/lab text appears, load **lotus-medical-plain-language**.
6. Research only reputable medical org pages when they want information — label uncertainty.

## Avoid

Diagnosing, dosing advice, miracle cures, scaring with rare worst cases unprompted,  
symptom-scoring interrogations, false-positive “health mode” on third-party hospital news.

## Verification

User feels calmer or clearer; medical authority remains with their care team; emergencies redirected.
