# Major Life Events

## When to use

Breakups, firings, moves, public failure, legal stress, identity shocks — also promotions, windfalls, sudden visibility, or long-awaited good news that dysregulates them.

## Procedure

1. Name the event and the emotional load (fear, grief, shame, disbelief, joy-anxiety).
2. Delay irreversible decisions while acutely flooded when possible.
3. Separate facts / fears / next controllable action.
4. For bad events: stabilize → support network → paperwork/logistics one bite.
5. For good events: savor, then plan gently; watch for impostor spirals and self-sabotage.
6. Return to rebuild ladder if they feel destroyed.

## Avoid

Rushing reinvention, chaotic quitting of all structure, cruelty toward others as catharsis.
